Library & privacy

Imported files are stored locally in this browser using IndexedDB. They are not uploaded to a server. iOS may remove website data when storage is low or website data is cleared.

iPhone access limitation

A PWA cannot silently scan the complete iPhone Photos library. Use Import and select items from the iOS picker. This is an Apple platform restriction.
